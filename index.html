    💬 When someone needs something they wave their arms and a bubble counts down 45s. Walk up to them, press E (or tap) and send them to the right room: the dining table, the powder room, a bed, the bath, the games corner, the meditation alcove… The roster on the right tells you which room they are in.
    💸 Human needs cost $5, pet needs $1, rising +$1 every minute (capped at $10). Miss 2 needs → they die 💀. Timers pause while they walk.
    🎂 Everyone ages 1 year every 10s. At 100 they pass away peacefully 😇.
    💼 Send anyone aged 12–60 to the home office desk: every 10s earns $5. Workers pay 50% for their needs, get a $1 raise every 5 years worked, and walk back to the desk on their own after a snack. After 60s they need a break on the sofa ☕.
    🐾 Pets only need food (kitchen bowls) and exercise (balcony). Leo, Cyrus and Pebble otherwise live their own lives: napping, grooming, playing together. Throw Leo's bone for fetch. 🤭 Talk to anyone with no needs to tickle them.
    🏠 The house still works: play a record, watch a movie in the window lounge, light the hallway candle, tidy the shoes, open the fridge, run the taps and the stove, call the lift, and try the claw machine and pinball.
    🧟 Press Z if you dare… a zombie comes through the front door. You have 10s to offer someone — or it picks!
    ⭐ Score: every second alive earns 1 point per living family member. P pauses.
